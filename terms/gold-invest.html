01What Gold Invest is

Gold Invest is a convenience feature inside the ZenMode OS launcher. It lets you choose a quantity of Gold BeES units and then hands you off to Zerodha Kite, where you review and confirm the order yourself.

That hand-off is the whole feature. ZenMode prepares an order intent and opens Kite. Everything after that point, including know-your-customer checks, order placement, payment, execution, settlement, custody, statements and taxation reporting, is done by your broker under your own agreement with them.

ZenMode is not a stock broker, sub-broker, authorised person, exchange, depository, portfolio manager, investment adviser or research analyst, and is not registered with the Securities and Exchange Board of India in any capacity.

03Eligibility

To use Gold Invest you must be at least 18 years old, be legally capable of entering into a contract, and be resident in India for the purposes of the applicable securities and exchange control rules.

You must already hold an active trading and demat account with the Broker, with KYC completed and in good standing. ZenMode does not open accounts, complete KYC, or verify your eligibility. If you do not have an account, the hand-off will simply take you to the Broker's own onboarding, which is governed entirely by them.

04How the hand-off works

The sequence is deliberately short and you can stop at any point:

  • You select a quantity of Gold BeES units inside ZenMode.
  • ZenMode opens Kite and passes the instrument and the quantity you chose as a pre-filled order intent.
  • You authenticate with the Broker, review the order, and decide whether to place it.
  • If you place it, the Broker executes, settles and credits the units to your demat account.

You are responsible for reviewing the order in Kite before confirming it, including the instrument, quantity, order type and price. ZenMode cannot place, modify, cancel or track orders on your behalf, and has no ability to move funds or units.

05We hold no money and no units

ZenMode does not accept, hold, pool, route or refund your money at any stage. Payment is made by you to the Broker through the Broker's own payment channels.

ZenMode does not hold your Gold BeES units, has no custody of them, and cannot pledge, transfer or sell them. Units are credited to your demat account, held in your own name, and remain yours regardless of whether you continue to use ZenMode.

If you uninstall ZenMode, close your account, or we withdraw the feature, your holdings are untouched. They live with your broker and depository, not with us.

10Risks you are accepting

Investing in a gold ETF carries risk, and you can lose money. In particular:

  • Price risk. The value of Gold BeES moves with the price of gold, which is volatile and can fall sharply and for long periods.
  • No assured return. There is no guaranteed, promised or protected return, and past performance says nothing about the future.
  • Tracking error. An ETF may not perfectly track the underlying gold price, because of fund expenses, cash holdings and operational factors.
  • Liquidity and impact cost. Market liquidity varies. The price you pay or receive may differ from the last traded price or the fund's net asset value.
  • Currency and policy risk. Domestic gold prices are affected by exchange rates, import duty and other policy decisions outside anyone's control.
  • Counterparty and platform risk. Execution depends on your Broker, the exchange and the depository, none of which ZenMode controls.

Do not invest money you cannot afford to lose, and do not treat a behavioural streak inside a launcher as a reason to take on financial risk you would otherwise avoid.

Always verify the instrument and quantity inside Kite before you confirm. Once you confirm an order in Kite it is between you and your Broker, and ZenMode cannot reverse it, cancel it, or claim compensation for it on your behalf.
