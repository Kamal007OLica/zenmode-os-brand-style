01The short version

ZenMode OS reads how you use your phone, which is about as sensitive as data gets. So the design rule is simple: that data is processed on your device and stays there.

What leaves your device is deliberately thin: the account details needed to pair you with an accountability partner, the Zen Score you agreed to share with them, and whatever you send us when you ask for help. We do not sell data, we do not run ads, and we do not have a business model that improves when you stay on your phone longer.

03What stays on your device

The following is processed locally and is not transmitted to us:

  • Raw Android usage statistics: which apps were opened, when, and for how long.
  • Your declared intents and session lengths.
  • The per-app categorisation you set, including which apps you tag as unintentional or productive.
  • The calculation that turns all of the above into your Zen Score and Zen Report.

If you never pair with a partner and never contact support, ZenMode can run without sending us your usage data at all.

05What we never collect

  • The content of your messages, emails, notifications or calls.
  • Keystrokes, screen contents or screenshots.
  • Your browsing history or the content of anything you read.
  • Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • Your broker credentials, portfolio, holdings or trades.

ZenMode contains no advertising identifiers and no third party advertising or analytics trackers. [CONFIRM AGAINST SHIPPED BUILD]

06Permissions, and why each one exists

  • Usage access. To read app usage statistics so Zen Score can be calculated on device. Without it there is no score.
  • Default home app. So ZenMode can act as your launcher.
  • Display over other apps. To show the intent prompt before an app opens.
  • Notifications. To send your daily Zen Report and session reminders.
  • Network access. For pairing, score sharing, updates and the Gold Invest hand-off.

You can revoke any of these in Android settings at any time. Revoking usage access disables Zen Score rather than silently degrading it.

07What your accountability partner can see

A paired partner sees your Zen Score, its direction against the previous day, and the summary in your Zen Report. The point of the design is that they see the number, not the shame.

They do not see your raw usage logs, the content of anything you do, or app-level detail beyond what your Zen Report itemises. You can unpair at any time, which stops all further sharing immediately.

08Gold Invest and your broker

Gold Invest hands you off to Zerodha Kite. The hand-off carries only the instrument and the quantity you selected. It does not carry your identity, your ZenMode account, your Zen Score, or any usage data.

We never receive your broker credentials, your holdings, your order history or your portfolio value. Once you are in Kite, your broker's own privacy policy governs what happens to your data, and we have no visibility into it.

09Why we process what we process

We process personal data to provide the features you asked for, in particular accounts and partner pairing; to keep the service secure and working; to fix defects; to respond to you when you contact us; and to comply with law. Where consent is the basis, you can withdraw it, and where a feature depends on it, withdrawing consent turns that feature off rather than the whole app.
