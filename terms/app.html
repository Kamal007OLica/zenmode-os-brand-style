What each permission is used for is set out in the Privacy Policy.

06Zen Score and Zen Report

Zen Score is a behavioural signal we calculate from your declared intents, session lengths and app usage. It starts at 100 each day, is reduced by unintentional usage, and is earned back through declared sessions that end on time and time in apps you have marked as productive.

The score is an approximation. Android usage statistics are imperfect, devices differ, and our rules are a judgement call rather than a measurement of anything objective. We may change how the score is calculated, and scores are not comparable across app versions.

Zen Score has no monetary value, cannot be transferred, sold or exchanged, and gives you no entitlement to any money, gold, unit or reward from us.
