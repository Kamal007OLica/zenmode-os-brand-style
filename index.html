THE ONE QUESTION

If there's a hook, why can't we unhook it?

That's ZenMode. Reverse engineered, hook-less. The unhooking is the product.

01 · THE ONE THING

One number that can go up.

Most screen-time tools count down on you. ZenMode gives you a score you earn back, a person who sees it, and a reward worth keeping.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

To help people reduce unintentional and meaningless phone use by creating an environment that makes intentional use easier, while providing accountability and rewards for healthier behaviour.

PROBLEM Unintentional and meaningless phone use.
SOLUTION An environment that makes intentional use easier.
USP Accountability and a rewards system for healthier behaviour.
POSITIONING

People don't need to abandon their phones. They need a better relationship with them.

ZenMode turns mindless phone use into intentional engagement through mindfulness, accountability and positive rewards.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Not a blocker. Not a detox. It will not shame you, cap your minutes, or treat every hour on a screen as a failure.

02 · HOW IT WORKS

Three moving parts.

01 · INTENT

Say why you opened it

Declare a session and a length before the app opens. The friction is the feature, and it lasts about two seconds.

02 · ZEN SCORE

Start at 100, earn it back

Unintentional pickups deduct. Declared sessions that end on time and productive app use earn back. The floor is 0, the ceiling is 100.

03 · ZEN REPORT

One receipt a day

Every deduction and gain, itemised with minutes attached. Your accountability partner sees the number and the direction, nothing else.

03 · ZEN SCORE

Accountability without the guilt.

Pair with one other ZenMode user through Random Connect. No social graph, no mutual friends, no feed. Just one person who sees your number move, which turns out to be all the pressure anyone needs.

DEDUCTS

Pickups with no intent set. Sessions past the length you asked for. Apps you tagged as unintentional.

EARNS BACK

Declared sessions that end on time. Productive app time. Streaks where intent matched behaviour.

04 · GOLD INVEST

Rewards you can't doomscroll.

A streak that turns into a badge is just dopamine wearing a different hat. So ZenMode points the habit at something that holds value instead: Gold BeES, the gold ETF traded on the NSE.

STEP 01

You pick the quantity

Choose how many units of Gold BeES you want. ZenMode shows you the number, never a recommendation.

STEP 02

Kite takes the order

You are handed off to Zerodha Kite. Your broker handles KYC, payment, execution, settlement and demat custody.

STEP 03

We hold nothing

ZenMode never touches your money and never holds your units. The gold sits in your demat account, in your name.

ZenMode is not a broker, an exchange, or an investment adviser, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell. Gold BeES is a market-traded ETF: its value moves with the gold price and can fall. Read the Gold Invest terms before you use the feature.

05 · OPEN SOURCE

No dark patterns to hide.

A product that asks you to trust it with your attention should be readable. ZenMode OS is open source, all the way down, and your usage data stays on your device.

ON DEVICE

Usage stats are processed locally. Your partner sees a score, not a log.

NO ADS

Nothing in ZenMode is paid for by holding your attention longer.
